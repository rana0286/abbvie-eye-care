XEN GEL STENT

Indication: The XEN Glaucoma Treatment System is indicated for the management of refractory glaucomas, including cases where previous surgical treatment has failed, cases of primary open-angle glaucoma, and pseudoexfoliative or pigmentary glaucoma with open angles that are unresponsive to maximum tolerated medical therapy.

Important Safety Information: Contraindications include angle closure glaucoma where angle has not been surgically opened, neovascular or uveitic glaucoma, and retained foreign body. Warnings include implant visualization through conjunctiva. The most common adverse events reported include best corrected visual acuity loss of 2 lines or more (≥10 letters), hypotony, and needling/revision (at 12 months).
